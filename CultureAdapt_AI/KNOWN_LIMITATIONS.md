# Known Limitations

1. Cultural adaptation is not a substitute for native cultural review.
2. Default visual assets are local production cards, not AI-generated images.
3. Real image generation may require a paid image model.
4. SQLite/database persistence is not included in this small MVP.
5. Scanned PDFs require OCR.
6. Dialect output must be reviewed by a fluent speaker.
7. Continuity checks are intentionally conservative.
