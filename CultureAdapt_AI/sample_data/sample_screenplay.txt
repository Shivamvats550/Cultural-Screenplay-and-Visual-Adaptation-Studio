THE LAST LETTER

SCENE 1 - EXT. VILLAGE COURTYARD - MORNING

Amar, 24, sits near his mother Bebe. He wears a simple kurta and carries an old letter.

BEBE
You have been quiet since morning.

AMAR
There is something I need to tell you.

Amar looks at the letter but does not open it.

SCENE 2 - INT. FAMILY ROOM - EVENING

Amar enters with the same letter. His younger sister Simran is arranging books.

SIMRAN
Is that the letter you received yesterday?

AMAR
Yes. I am not ready to show it to Bebe.

Simran looks worried.

SCENE 3 - EXT. COURTYARD - NIGHT

Bebe waits outside. Amar finally approaches her.

AMAR
Bebe, I should have told you earlier.

He hands the letter to her.

Bebe reads it silently.

BEBE
Whatever happens, we will face it together.
